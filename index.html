Plato

Private assistant for Aristotle Industries

Plato is a private, single-user assistant built for Aristotle Industries. It helps the owner keep track of business email, orders, and day-to-day operations, and reports to the owner through Telegram.

Plato is not a public product. It is not available for sign-up, and it only connects to accounts belonging to Aristotle Industries and its owner.
